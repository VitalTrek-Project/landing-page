The Problem

Adventure tourism has real risks.

The agencies manage remote groups using radios and phone calls. The trekkers get lost, suffer altitude sickness, or experience medical emergencies with no way to alert others in time. There's no tracking, no history, and no digital experience to build tourist loyalty.

How it works

From worry to certainty.

The same story, every trek: a group sets off, something happens on the trail, and the right people find out in time to help.

  1. 01

    The expedition begins

    Your tourists set off on the trail wearing their vitals monitor and offline map, connected to you from minute one.

  2. 02

    An alert is detected

    A heart rate spike, an unexpected stop, a lost signal. VitalTrek notices before it becomes an emergency.

  3. 03

    Help arrives on time

    Your team gets the exact location and context to respond, and your tourist gets home safe.

Technology

VitalTrek IoT Ecosystem

An integrated ecosystem of hardware and software that captures, synchronizes, and displays data from each adventure in real time or offline.

IoT Wearables

Compact devices that capture pulse, SpO₂, body temperature and GPS location with long battery life.

Bluetooth Checkpoints

Beacons installed on key routes that record the passage of each tourist and relay data even without WiFi.

Mobile App

Offline navigation, experience recording, and contextual tour information for tourists.

Web Platform

Dashboard for agencies with live maps, automatic alerts and detailed reports for each tour.

Who are we

We are a team passionate about safe adventure.

VitalTrek was born out of frustration at seeing adventure tourism grow without the technological tools it deserves. We are five Peruvian engineers who believe that exploring the world should be a free, connected, and safe experience for everyone.

Who we are

An IoT technology startup

VitalTrek is a platform that integrates wearable hardware, smart checkpoints, and cloud-based software to transform the management of adventure tours in Peru and Latin America.

Mission

Connect adventure with safety

Our mission is to provide tourists and agencies with the necessary technological tools to enjoy adventure experiences with real-time information, preventing risks and preserving the excitement of the unknown.

Vision

The global standard for smart tourism

By 2028, to be the leading platform in Latin America for adventure tour management, expanding our IoT network to the continent's main trekking routes.

Frequently asked questions

Questions? We're happy to answer them.

What happens to my tourists' data?

It's encrypted and used only to keep your tours safe, we never sell it. Full details in our Privacy Policy.

Do you help us implement it?

Yes. Every agency plan includes onboarding, guide training, and initial hardware setup.

Does it work without internet signal?

Yes. VitalTrek is built for the backcountry: it stores data locally and syncs automatically the moment a signal is available.

What if a tourist has a real medical emergency?

VitalTrek is a monitoring and alert tool, not a replacement for emergency services, but it gives your guide the information they need to respond fast.

Can I try it before paying?

Yes. The Trekker and Pro plans include a 7-day free trial, no strings attached.
